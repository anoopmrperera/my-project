How it works & tips

Microphone mode listens only for the scratchy, high-pitched sound of a pen moving on paper. It measures how loud that sound is, many times a second, and marks each moment as writing or quiet. No audio is recorded or kept.

Motion mode uses the phone's movement sensors. Strap the phone firmly to the forearm of your writing hand (a sports armband works). Small hand movements while writing are counted as writing.

  • Work in a quiet room. Talking, music and typing will be picked up.
  • A pencil or ballpoint on paper over a hard table gives the clearest sound.
  • Keep the screen on and the app open while recording.
  • Breaks between letters and words are part of a burst. A pause is a longer break (1 second by default, see Settings).
  • Steadiness (0–100) shows how evenly sized your bursts and pauses were. High means a regular, even rhythm; low means a stop-and-go rhythm with very uneven pauses.

🔒 Everything stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.
